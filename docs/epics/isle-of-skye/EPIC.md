# Epic: Isle of Skye

[← Master plan](../../PLAN.md) · **Status: ⏳ Not started** · Effort: **high**

## Goal

Isle of Skye on the platform: 2–5 clan chiefs price tiles in secret, buy each other's tiles and build their kingdoms, scoring changing goals each round.

**What it adds to the platform:** up to 5 seats; simultaneous secret pricing (`simultaneous` actors, commit then reveal); tiles drawn from a hidden bag; scoring and a final ranking (a competitive `outcome`).

**Licensing:** a published, trademarked game. Fine for the private, invite-only site; a public site needs the publisher's permission or an original game.

**Epic done when:** 2–5 players finish a game on phones and desktop, with all its rules tested.
